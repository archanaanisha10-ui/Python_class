3.Write a program to input the length and breadth of a rectangle and calculate its area and perimeter.
 Area and perimeter of rectangle
length = float(input("Enter length: "))
breadth = float(input("Enter breadth: "))

area = length * breadth
perimeter = 2 * (length + breadth)

print("Area:", area)
print("Perimeter:", perimeter)

 
4.Write a program to convert temperature from Celsius to Fahrenheit.
  Celsius to Fahrenheit
celsius = float(input("Enter temperature in Celsius: "))

fahrenheit = (celsius * 9 / 5) + 32

print("Temperature in Fahrenheit:", fahrenheit)

5.Write a program to input a student's name and marks in three subjects and display the total and average.
  Student total and average
name = input("Enter student name: ")

mark1 = float(input("Enter mark for subject 1: "))
mark2 = float(input("Enter mark for subject 2: "))
mark3 = float(input("Enter mark for subject 3: "))

total = mark1 + mark2 + mark3
average = total / 3

print("Student Name:", name)
print("Total:", total)
print("Average:", average)

8.Write a program to calculate simple interest using principal, rate, and time.

 Simple interest
principal = float(input("Enter principal: "))
rate = float(input("Enter rate: "))
time = float(input("Enter time: "))

simple_interest = (principal * rate * time) / 100

print("Simple Interest:", simple_interest)
